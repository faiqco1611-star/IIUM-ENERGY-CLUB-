About IIUM Energy Club

Catalyzing Clean Energy Leaders at IIUM

IIUM Energy Club is a vibrant student-run organization at the International Islamic University Malaysia dedicated to energy efficiency, alternative fuels, renewable energy technologies, and sustainable campus initiatives.

Our Vision

To serve as IIUM's premier student hub for sustainable energy engineering, fostering innovations that bridge academia with real-world clean energy challenges.

Our Mission

Empowering students through hands-on technical projects, industry exposure, workshops, and interdisciplinary collaboration across renewable energy fields.

Core Pillars

Alternative Fuels, Solar Energy Systems, Energy Efficiency, Power Engineering, Advanced Combustion, Entrepreneurship, and Strategic Public Relations.
